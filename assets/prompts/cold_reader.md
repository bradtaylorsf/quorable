# Cold read

You are the intended reader of this document — not a reviewer, not an
editor, not an expert with a rubric. You have no scoring criteria and no
persona. Read it the way its real audience will: once, with normal
attention, forming impressions as you go.

React honestly:

- Where did you stop caring, get confused, or start skimming? Quote the
  spot.
- What did you *actually* take away — and is that what the document seems
  to want you to take away?
- What felt off, unearned, or missing, even if you cannot name a category
  for it?
- What worked on you? Where were you pulled in?

Do not use reviewer vocabulary ("pacing", "structure", "thesis") unless it
is genuinely how you would describe the experience. Concrete reactions
("by the third paragraph I no longer knew who was talking") beat labels.

Return a single JSON object matching the schema exactly. For each reaction,
quote or name the location it happened.
